Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026 · In case of conflict, the Turkish version prevails.

In short
  • No account required. We do not collect your name, email, phone number, location or photos.
  • The game has no ads, we do not track you across apps and we never sell your data.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • Only if you make an in-app purchase, an anonymous ID and the purchase record are sent to our payment infrastructure provider RevenueCat to validate it.

1. Who we are

This policy applies to the iOS game Altın Nesil: Football Academy (the “Game”). The data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) and Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (“KVKK”) is the developer who publishes the Game on the App Store, Bilal Iyisoy.

Contact: keybridgeapp@gmail.com

2. What data we process

2.1 Data that stays on your device

The following is stored on your device and is not sent to us or to third parties:

  • Game progress: club name and crest, squad, facilities, league and season status, coins, settings (sound, haptics, notifications, commentary).
  • Age range: obtained through iOS's Declared Age Range feature only if you or your parent choose to share it. We receive a range (e.g. “18 or over”), never a birth date. It is used on your device only to show age-appropriate special offers.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled locally on your device. We have no notification server and collect no push token.
  • Crash diagnostics: Apple's MetricKit creates crash and hang summaries on your device. The Game keeps them on the device and does not send them to us.

Deleting the app removes all of this data from your device. You can also erase your progress at any time via Profile → Reset Progress.

2.2 In-app purchases (RevenueCat)

Apple processes payments; we never see your card or payment details. To validate, deliver and restore purchases, our purchase infrastructure provider RevenueCat, Inc. (USA) processes:

  • A random, anonymous user ID that RevenueCat generates for your device. It is not linked to your name, email or Apple Account.
  • Purchase records: Apple receipt, product, date and transaction details.
  • Technical information: device type, operating system version, language/region and currency, time the app was last used.

This data is sent to RevenueCat only when you purchase or restore purchases. RevenueCat processes it on our behalf and on our instructions (as a processor). See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

2.3 Apple services

  • Game Center: if you are signed in to Game Center, your Game Center nickname and score appear on leaderboards. This service is provided by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can turn Game Center off in iOS Settings.
  • Friendly matches (Game Center): when you invite a Game Center friend to a friendly or accept an invite, your club name, crest and a snapshot of your match squad (player names, positions, ratings) plus the match result are stored on Apple's Game Center servers and are visible only to that friend. This data does not come to us. There is no matchmaking with strangers and no chat. Offensive words in club names are hidden automatically. From the result screen you can report a club name to us; the name is hidden on your device immediately. You can also block a player. The list of players you block or hide is kept only on your device and can be cleared at any time from the Friendly Match card.
  • App Store analytics: if you enabled “Share with App Developers” on iOS, Apple may share aggregated, anonymous usage and crash statistics with us. You control this in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

2.4 What we do not collect

Name, email, phone, address, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, health data, advertising identifier (IDFA). The Game has no ads or ad tracking and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. There is no chat or messaging between players.

When you report a club name, your email app prepares a message to us containing that club name and the match ID. We use it only to review the report.

4. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising (including “sale” or “sharing” as defined by U.S. state privacy laws such as the CCPA/CPRA). Purchase data is shared only with our processor RevenueCat, which stores data in Amazon Web Services data centers in the USA; making a purchase therefore involves a transfer outside your country. Transfers rely on appropriate safeguards under GDPR Chapter V and KVKK Art. 9 (RevenueCat's data processing agreement and standard contractual clauses).

We may disclose data to competent authorities where required by law.

5. Retention

  • On-device data: until you delete the app or reset your progress.
  • Purchase records (RevenueCat): as long as needed to let you restore purchases and to meet legal retention obligations (e.g. tax and accounting law); then deleted or anonymized.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after your request is resolved.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests, to data portability, and to know who your data was shared with (GDPR Arts. 15–21; KVKK Art. 11; similar rights under U.S. state laws).

How to make a request: email keybridgeapp@gmail.com. Because our user IDs are anonymous, please include the order ID from the purchase receipt Apple emailed you so we can locate your purchase data. We respond free of charge within 30 days.

You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority).

7. Children's privacy

The Game is suitable for football fans of all ages. We do not collect names, emails or contact details from anyone of any age; there are no accounts, chat or ads. Players known to be under 18, or whose age is unknown, are not shown time-limited or personalized special offers; players under 13 are not shown any special offers. Purchases can require parental approval through Apple's Family Sharing and Ask to Buy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (COPPA). If you believe there is an issue regarding your child, contact us and we will act promptly.

8. Security

We minimize risk by collecting as little personal data as possible. On-device data is protected by iOS app sandboxing and encryption; communication with RevenueCat uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

9. Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the effective date and announce material changes in the Game. Previous versions are available on request at keybridgeapp@gmail.com.
