Frequently asked questions

My purchase didn't arrive / disappeared on a new phone

Tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the Store tab. Permanent items are restored and any undelivered purchase is delivered. If the problem persists, email us with the order ID from the receipt Apple emailed you.

I want a refund

Apple processes all payments, so refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Your progress is stored on your device. When you move to a new iPhone via iCloud backup or device-to-device transfer, your save comes along.

I want to start over

Profile → Reset Progress. Permanent items you purchased are kept.

I want to turn off notifications

Profile → Settings → Notifications, or on iPhone Settings → Notifications → Altın Nesil.

My child plays — how do I control purchases?

Turn on Ask to Buy in Apple's Family Sharing and every purchase will wait for your approval. The game has no ads and no chat. Details: Children's privacy.

I want my data deleted

The game requires no account and collects no personal information. Deleting the app removes all data from your device. To have purchase records deleted, follow the steps in the Privacy Policy.
